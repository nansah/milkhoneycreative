Productivity Web App

DreamCatcher

A goal-tracking app that helps you capture your dreams, break them into actionable steps, and stay motivated every day until you get there.

Type Productivity App
Role Design & Development
Focus Goal Setting, UX
Live dreamcatcher-tan.vercel.app

The Problem

Most people have dreams. Few have a real system for achieving them. The productivity app space is crowded — but most tools focus on tasks, not transformation. They help you manage what's urgent, not what matters most. DreamCatcher was built to close that gap: a space where your biggest ambitions are taken seriously.

The Concept

DreamCatcher is built on a simple but powerful idea: your goals deserve more than a bullet point on a to-do list. The app gives your dreams structure — breaking them into milestones, tracking daily progress, and surrounding you with accountability so you never lose momentum or sight of why you started.

Key Features

  • Goal Command Center — Dashboard view with active goals, overall progress, and today's focus tasks at a glance
  • Day Streak Tracker — Daily check-ins build streaks that make consistency feel rewarding
  • Milestone Breakdown — Each goal is broken into concrete steps with timeline and time commitment
  • Accountability Circle — Invite a partner to watch your goals and send "Nudges" when you need a push
  • Reminders — Gentle, timely prompts to keep you on track with daily progress
  • Warm, Focused UI — A cream and terracotta palette that feels calm and intentional — not stressful

The Dashboard

The dashboard is the heart of DreamCatcher. At a glance: your day streak, active goals, overall progress percentage, and how many accountability partners are watching. Below that — today's focus tasks, your weekly streak grid, your accountability circle, and your active goal cards with timeline and effort stats. It's designed to be the first thing you open in the morning and the last thing you check at night.

Design Approach

The interface uses a warm cream background with soft terracotta and orange accents — a palette that communicates optimism and focus without the coldness of most productivity tools. Cards are clean and rounded. Typography is clear and generous. Every visual decision was made to reduce anxiety around goals and replace it with quiet confidence.

The accountability circle feature was a deliberate product choice — goals are easier to keep when someone is watching. "Nudge" interactions are lightweight and human, not gamified noise.

Technology

DreamCatcher is a full-stack web application deployed on Vercel. The data layer supports real-time updates across users — so when your accountability partner completes a task or your streak changes, it reflects immediately. Authentication, data persistence, and notifications are all handled within a clean, scalable architecture.
